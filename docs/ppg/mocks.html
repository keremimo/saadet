PPG, the pages

Mocks of a PPG Wave 2.2 engine's EDIT pages on the panel's own 240 × 240 geometry, for review before any page is written in firmware. The header, the four cards and the faces follow ui_view.c, drawn with the helpers of docs/hydra-lab/mocks.html. The waves are stand-ins computed here. The unit draws the ROM's, and this public file holds none of PPG's data.

  • One part plays one group. A 2.2 program holds two sounds, groups A and B, and its keyboard modes 0..8 layer or split them over eight voices. Here a part is one group: layers and splits come from Saadet's two parts (MULTI), and a performance stores both. KEYB modes 1..8 then need no page of their own.
  • The 2.2's own numbers. Every knob shows 0..63 as the 2.2's ANALOG display did, stepped as the original quantises it (attack 16 steps, emphasis 16, decay/sustain/release 32, cutoff and the waves 64). Old patch sheets then read the same. ENV 3's amount is shown signed (−32..+31) instead of the 2.2's 32-centred number.
  • Stepping, not crossfading. The wave number moves in whole waves, as on the 2.2; the blends between the ROM's key waves are built when a table loads (the "over 1800 waves"), not while a note plays. Slots 60..63 are triangle, pulse, square and saw in every table.
  • Velocity is MIDI velocity. The 2.2 had no velocity keyboard: its VELOSITY effect took the pressure on the first held key. VF/VL here follow the note's own velocity.
  • Open, from the OS code later: how tables 28 (phasing saw) and 29 (pulse widths) are computed, the LFO's four shapes (the manual does not name them, so they show as 1..4), and the envelope and rate curves. Table 31 (piano and sax samples) is not in the wave image; TABLE stops at UPPER until those samples are found.
  • For Kerem: the name on the engine list (PPG here), its colour (cyan here, beside FM6's blue and HYDRA's green), the page order, and whether the groups-as-parts reading holds.
